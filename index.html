NUNA Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 6, 2026
NUNA is a wellness application for general wellness and personal organization. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional medical advice.

NUNA (“NUNA”, “we”, “our”, or “the App”) is a wellness application designed to help users organize personal wellness information, including body-state reflections, food and dietary information, medication and supplement records, reminders, documents, daily check-ins, and related wellness information.

1. Information NUNA Processes

Depending on the features you use, NUNA may process information you enter into the App, including:

  • daily wellness check-ins such as energy, sleep, stress, digestion, and temperature sensations;
  • Body-State questionnaire responses and results;
  • safety or warning-sign questionnaire responses;
  • food records;
  • dietary preferences, food exclusions, disliked foods, and dietary patterns;
  • allergy information and other self-reported health considerations;
  • medication and supplement names, doses, schedules, purposes, notes, and adherence history;
  • reminders and reminder activity;
  • documents and records that you choose to add;
  • locally entered feedback and comments;
  • App settings and preferences.

Some of this information may be considered personal, sensitive, or health-related information.

2. How NUNA Uses Information

NUNA uses information you provide to support App functionality, including:

  • displaying your wellness records;
  • generating non-diagnostic Body-State summaries;
  • adapting food prioritization and wellness-related suggestions;
  • applying safety filtering where appropriate;
  • displaying medication or supplement schedules;
  • creating local reminders;
  • showing trends derived from your saved information;
  • organizing documents and personal records;
  • creating and restoring backups when requested by you.

NUNA does not use your information to diagnose diseases or determine the medical cause of symptoms.

3. Local Storage

NUNA is designed primarily as a local-first application. Most user-entered information is stored on your device in the App's private local database and private application storage.

NUNA does not currently provide cloud accounts, remote synchronization, developer-hosted cloud storage, Firebase synchronization, user login, or account creation.

The current version does not automatically upload your wellness or health information to NUNA Studio servers.

4. Android System Backup

Android may provide operating-system-level backup or device migration features. Depending on your Android device settings, Google account settings, Android version, and device manufacturer, some application data may be included in Android system backup or device-transfer mechanisms.

This functionality is provided by the Android operating system and is not NUNA's own cloud synchronization service.

You can manage Android backup settings through your device and Google account settings.

5. NUNA Backup and Restore

NUNA allows users to intentionally create encrypted backup files. NUNA backup files use encryption and may contain locally stored App data and documents.

When creating a backup, you choose where the file is saved through the system file picker. Depending on the services available on your device, you may choose local device storage, Google Drive, OneDrive, or another compatible storage provider.

NUNA does not automatically upload these backup files. If you save a backup to a third-party cloud provider, that provider processes the file according to its own privacy policy.

6. External Recipe Searches

NUNA may allow you to search for external recipes using your device's web browser.

When you activate this feature, NUNA may create a search query containing a food or ingredient name, your selected dietary pattern (such as vegan, vegetarian, or Buddhist vegetarian), and a general recipe-related search term.

NUNA does not include Body-State questionnaire answers, Body-State results, medication records, red-flag responses, allergy notes, daily check-ins, or other detailed health records in these recipe-search queries.

The search is opened in an external browser. The browser, search engine, or external website may independently process information such as your IP address, browser cookies, Google account information, search history, and device or browser information. These external services are governed by their own privacy policies.

NUNA does not receive or store your external browsing history or search results.

7. Notifications

NUNA may provide local notifications for medication, supplements, or other reminders. These notifications are generated on your device and do not rely on a NUNA push-notification server.

Depending on your notification and lock-screen settings, notification content may display information such as a medication or supplement name or dose. You can control notification visibility and permissions through your Android device settings.

8. Biometric Authentication

NUNA includes technical support for device-based biometric authentication. If biometric authentication is used, authentication is performed by the Android operating system.

NUNA does not receive, store, or process your fingerprint or facial biometric template. NUNA only receives the authentication result supplied by the device.

9. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

The current version of NUNA does not use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Google Analytics, advertising SDKs, behavioral advertising, advertising identifiers, or developer-operated telemetry services.

NUNA does not sell personal or health-related information and does not use health-related information for advertising or advertising profiling.

10. Sharing of Information

NUNA does not automatically share your personal health information with advertisers or data brokers.

Information may leave the App when you intentionally save an encrypted backup to an external storage provider, open an external recipe search, intentionally select or save files through Android's system file picker, when Android system backup or device migration operates according to your device settings, or when disclosure is required by applicable law.

11. Permissions

NUNA may request or use Android permissions for showing local notifications, restoring scheduled reminders after device restart, notification vibration, and device biometric authentication.

NUNA does not currently request access to precise or approximate location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, Health Connect, or broad device storage access.

Files and documents are accessed through Android's system file picker when you explicitly choose them.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored inside NUNA generally remains on your device until it is changed, deleted through an available App function, cleared through Android settings, or removed when the App is uninstalled.

Certain records may currently have different deletion behavior. For example, food entries may be deleted; documents added to NUNA may be deleted; medication records may be marked inactive or finished while historical adherence records may remain; some Body-State and Daily Check-in history may not currently have an individual delete control.

You may remove the App's locally stored data by using Android's Clear storage / Clear data function or by uninstalling NUNA.

Files that you separately exported or saved outside the App, including .nuna backup files, are not automatically deleted when NUNA is uninstalled. Android system backups may also be managed separately through Android or Google account backup settings.

13. Security

NUNA uses application-private storage for its local database and files. NUNA-created .nuna backup files are encrypted before being saved.

However, no device, storage system, operating system, or electronic transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your device, device account, lock-screen credentials, and exported backup files.

14. Children's Privacy

NUNA is not specifically designed for children. If NUNA is made available to children or minors in the future, appropriate consent, privacy, safety, and Google Play requirements will be implemented before release.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when NUNA's features, data practices, legal requirements, or Google Play requirements change. The updated Privacy Policy will show a revised effective date.
